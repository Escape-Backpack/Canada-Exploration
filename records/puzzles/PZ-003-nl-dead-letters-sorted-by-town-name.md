## How it works
Idea only, nothing decided (2026-10-22). Source: *The Long Way Home* mockup (`the-long-way-home.html`, external, see HANDOFF 2026-10-03). Numbers below are the mockup's values, not envelope answers: envelope answers must be 4-5 characters.
- Six letters written in Newfoundland English. A slang dictionary and a gazetteer of odd town names (Come By Chance, Blow Me Down, Heart's Content/Delight/Desire, Witless Bay, Happy Adventure) tell which town each writer lives in.
- Filing each letter in its town's pigeonhole puts the pictures on the envelope backs together: a cod showing a registration number.
- Mockup props: 6 letters, dictionary, gazetteer, 10-hole sorting rack with decoy cards. Mockup answer: 61.
- The dictionary also carries the Screech-In reply used in the mockup's ending.
