Idea (2026-10-22, designer). A postcard in the parents' voice from Churchill, MB, for PZ-009. It carries both the story and the 6 release rules (designer, 2026-10-22; replaces the separate notice card PP-003).

**Format (designer, 2026-10-22):** not the Norse postcard format. One art side and one open text side: notes from the parents only, no Fun Fact box, no address.

**Text (designer's wording, with all 5 suggested edits accepted 2026-10-22):**

> Greetings from Churchill!
> It is the Polar Bear Capital of the World. They even have a polar bear jail here for any bear that strolls into town. They get locked up until the officers here release them in the wild.
> While we were touring the facility, the officers had to move a mother and cub, a young male and an old male. They couldn't release them all in the same spot, so they had some rules to ensure they could go their separate ways and each end up in a good spot. It was a pretty neat puzzle to solve. Can you do as well as the officers?
> 1. Every bear goes on open tundra: not in town, not on the bay, not at the dump.
> 2. No two bears in neighbouring squares, diagonals included.
> 3. Mother and cub at least three squares from any part of town. A diagonal step counts as one.
> 4. The old male waits on the shore, in a square that shares a side with the bay.
> 5. The old male goes east of the mother and cub.
> 6. The young male goes next to the old dump. He'd find his way there anyway.
>
> Mom & Dad

**Fit check (2026-10-22):** about 200 words. On a 6 x 4 in text side with 0.3 in margins, Nothing You Could Do at 11.5 pt runs 1 line over; 10.5 pt fits. The designer wants a different handwriting font for Canada.

**Font (designer, 2026-10-22): Indie Flower** (Google Fonts, SIL OFL), chosen over Caveat, Kalam, Patrick Hand, Shadows Into Light Two and Gochi Hand. Not used in Norse or Space. File: `EscapeBackpack/Fonts/Indie_Flower/IndieFlower-Regular.ttf`. Measured fit: 11 pt with 1.3 line spacing on the 6 x 4 in text side (0.3 in margins). Not paper-tested.

**Art side (designer, 2026-10-22):** the watercolour travel sketch, option 2 from the four concepts, is selected. Current art: AS-012.

Open: a line saying which bear(s) give the code, once PZ-009's answer format is chosen; print/bleed preparation; which envelope it comes in; Q-008.
