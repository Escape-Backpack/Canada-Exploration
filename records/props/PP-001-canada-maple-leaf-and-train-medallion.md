## Selected design
The first stylised leaf was rejected on 2026-10-05; v2 uses the existing maple-leaf SVG outline.
On 2026-10-05 the designer requested this medallion and selected maple leaf and train.
Navy body, yellow front relief, with the same improved shared back used on Space,
Norse and Hiking. The motif and digital design are selected; physical manufacture
and print quality remain untested. The exact final-lock release and physical
container are not assigned by this session.

## Player sees
A themed keepsake with maple leaf and train on the front and
ADVENTURE / COMPLETE / ESCAPE BACKPACK around a compass on the engraved back.
Dimensions, measurements and manufacturing files live in AS-003.

## Starting state
Intended as a final prize; placement awaits the game's completed release sequence.
