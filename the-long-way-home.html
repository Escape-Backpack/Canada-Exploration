The Long Way Home

How to play. Open the pockets. Every souvenir resolves to a two-digit number. Look that cell up on the province's passport page to get her stamp. Thirteen stamps feed the two puzzles on the flap, and those open the inner pocket. Her journal answers most questions in plain sight. When it doesn't, a coin from the purse opens a hint tab.
